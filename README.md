# ⭐ Featured Work

## 🥖 Bakery E-Commerce & Operations Engine

### A complete example of connecting a customer storefront with real business operations.

**Repository:**
https://github.com/Ashutosh-Neupane/bakery-ecommerce-ops-engine

This project demonstrates how a traditional ordering process can be transformed into a connected digital workflow.

### Customer Experience

Customers can:

```text
Browse Products
      ↓
Choose Product
      ↓
Customize Order
      ↓
Select Pickup / Delivery
      ↓
Choose Available Time Slot
      ↓
Pay Deposit
      ↓
Receive Confirmation
```

### Business Operations

The order then moves into the operational workflow:

```text
Customer Order
      ↓
Payment Confirmation
      ↓
Order Created
      ↓
Production Planning
      ↓
Kitchen / Work Order
      ↓
BOM & Inventory
      ↓
Fulfillment
```

### Key Features

* 🛒 Modern e-commerce storefront
* 🎂 Product and cake ordering
* 📅 Pickup & delivery scheduling
* ⏱️ Time-slot capacity management
* 💳 Stripe Checkout
* 🔔 Stripe webhook synchronization
* 📦 Inventory integration
* 🧾 Automated order workflows
* 👨‍🍳 Kitchen preparation workflow
* 📋 Automated kitchen sheets
* ⚙️ ERPNext integration
* 📊 Operational data synchronization

### Technology

`Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `Tailwind CSS` · `Stripe` · `Frappe` · `ERPNext` · `Python` · `REST APIs`
